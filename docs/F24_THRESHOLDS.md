## English summary

Thresholds/confidence are currently set by defaults and rules-based confidence, not empirically calibrated.
F-24 requires a human-labeled gold set (F04) and held-out evaluation. Without it, no calibration claim can be
made. Thresholds remain versioned/limited and models stay advisory (not final decision makers).
